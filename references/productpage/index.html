Product overview

SYNLawn Classic 35 is a natural multi-tone synthetic lawn with a 35mm pile. COOLplus heat-reduction technology keeps the surface cooler on hot days, so it's comfortable for kids and pets.

It's soft underfoot, holds its colour year-round and needs no mowing, watering or fertiliser. A good all-round choice for family backyards, front lawns and entertaining areas.
